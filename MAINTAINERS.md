## Continuity

The Esheria Ventures Limited engineering lead is the backup maintainer role and
may restore repository access, appoint an acting maintainer, or coordinate an
orderly handover. Changes to named maintainers are recorded in this file and in
the public project governance log.

Domain review is distributed across privacy/security, safeguarding/product,
mobile/API, machine learning, legal/language, accessibility, and release roles.
No maintainer may self-approve a required independent review.

The canonical public repository is the community collaboration surface. A
private full-history integration repository retains restricted records and
production-only material; publication is performed through the reviewed,
checksum-ledgered process in `PUBLIC-MIRROR.md`.
