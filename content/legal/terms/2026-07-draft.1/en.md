# SafeRide terms — engineering review draft

Status: pending legal, safeguarding, and privacy review. This draft is not an
effective agreement and must not be presented for acceptance.

SafeRide is a support and reporting prototype. It is not an emergency service,
legal representative, evidence custodian, provider guarantee, or substitute for
professional advice. Provider listings and automated guidance can be incomplete
or stale. A person remains in control of whether to save, share, or submit a
report pathway.

Anonymous aggregation, partner delivery receipts, model-tuned safety claims,
processing for minors, public release, production deployment, and UNICEF
approval are not available unless their separate technical and human approval
gates pass.

The legally responsible entity, applicable jurisdiction, dispute terms,
eligibility, and effective date are intentionally absent pending legal review.
