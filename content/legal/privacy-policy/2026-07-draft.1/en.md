# SafeRide privacy policy — engineering review draft

Status: pending legal, safeguarding, and privacy review. This draft is not an
effective privacy notice and must not be presented for acceptance.

SafeRide can keep an encrypted report draft on this device. A person chooses
whether to keep it private, contact a selected provider, or submit an explicit
case workflow. Each choice has a separate data preview. Anonymous map signals,
research, model training, analytics, partner follow-up, processing for minors,
and automated server retention are unavailable until their own approvals and
technical gates pass.

Local exports and deletion cover only the stores named in the app. Deleting
local data cannot recall information already shared with another organisation.
Submitted-case access, correction, restriction, objection, export, and deletion
requests remain unavailable until the legally approved process is configured.

Evidence uploaded to SafeRide is quarantined. It cannot be downloaded until its
hash matches and an approved malware scan reports clean. Requested face blur,
metadata removal, or file encryption is not represented as complete without
the corresponding processing evidence.

This draft intentionally contains no contact address, controller identity,
lawful-basis decision, jurisdiction-specific retention promise, or children's
processing terms. Those fields require the legal handoff before publication.
