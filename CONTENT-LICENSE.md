# Content license status

No public content license is currently granted. CC BY 4.0 is a candidate only;
it must not be represented as approved until legal review identifies the exact
included content, attribution, exclusions, and effective revision.

User records, evidence, prompts, completions, participant material, partner
material, exact locations, credentials, restricted reviews, and security
reports are never public content under this notice.

The controlling state is config/release/open-source-policy.v1.json. Public
content redistribution remains disabled while that policy is blocked.
