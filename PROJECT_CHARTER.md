# SafeRide project charter

SafeRide aims to support privacy-preserving, survivor-centered reporting,
evidence handling, trusted referrals, and aggregate safety learning. It is not
an emergency service, legal representative, medical provider, or substitute
for human safeguarding and local support.

The project prioritizes user agency, data minimization, encryption, explicit
consent, truthful capability language, fail-closed AI/release controls,
synthetic development evidence, accessibility, and independent review.

Success is measured by verified safe behavior and reviewable evidence, not by
unsupported readiness claims. Field use, public release, model distribution,
partner operation, and UNICEF submission each require their own approvals.
