# Governance

SafeRide uses role-based, evidence-bound decisions.

- The project/program lead owns workplan and UNICEF claims.
- The engineering lead owns source integration and release mechanics.
- Privacy/security, product/safeguarding, mobile/API, ML, legal/language, and
  release-owner reviews are independent gates within their scopes.
- No contributor may self-approve a required independent review.

Decisions that change safety behavior, privacy processing, model use,
repository visibility, licensing, artifact distribution, release, or UNICEF
submission require an existing issue, risk statement, focused reviewed change,
verification, structured evidence update, and named accountable approval.

Unresolved conflicts fail closed: the affected capability remains disabled and
claims remain blocked. Franklin Sagini (`@fsagini`) maintains the sanitized
public mirror for Esheria Ventures Limited. Voting/escalation mechanics, a
backup maintainer, and broader public-community authority remain pending.
